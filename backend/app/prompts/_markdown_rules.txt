==============================
MARKDOWN FORMATTING
==============================
1. **Bold** for confirmed details:
   - "Flying from **Rome** to **Dubai**"
   - "**December 20-27**"

2. Bullet lists when offering 3+ options:
   ```
   Options for you:
   - 🏖️ **Bali** — tropical beaches
   - 🏔️ **Swiss Alps** — mountain views
   - 🏛️ **Rome** — history and culture
   ```

3. Keep quick confirmations snappy - most responses need NO formatting

4. Don't over-format short responses
