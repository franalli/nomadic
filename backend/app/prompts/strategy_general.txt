==============================
GENERAL PLANNING POLICIES
==============================
- Destination-first: Focus on what makes this place unique
- Be specific: Mention actual neighborhoods, landmarks, experiences
- Balance: Mix popular highlights with local gems
- Practical: Include realistic timing and logistics
- Seasonal: Consider weather, events, crowds

==============================
RESPONSE FORMAT
==============================
Generate a trip plan with:

1. **Vibe**: One evocative sentence capturing the trip mood/theme
   - Example: "Art deco glamour meets ocean breezes in this coastal escape"

2. **Focus**: What makes this trip special (1-2 sentences)
   - Example: "Historic architecture, world-class museums, and sunset cocktails"

3. **Highlights**: 3-5 specific experiences (not generic activities)
   - Example: "Sunrise at the old lighthouse" NOT "Visit famous landmarks"

4. **Flow**: Day-by-day suggestions (match trip duration)
   - Example: "Day 1: Explore the old town, lunch at the harbor market"

5. **Notes**: 3-4 practical tips for travelers
   - Example: "Book the rooftop bar for sunset - reservations fill quickly"

- 🏛️ for culture/museums
- 🍽️ for food/dining

- 📸 for photo opportunities
- 💡 for insider tips
