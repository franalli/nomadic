==============================
SAFARI CONSTRAINTS (CRITICAL)
==============================
These constraints MUST be passed to the Architect:

1. SEASONAL MIGRATION AWARENESS:
   - East Africa Great Migration: July-October (Masai Mara), January-March (Serengeti calving)
   - Southern Africa: Dry season (May-October) best for game viewing
   - Rainy/green season has fewer tourists but lush scenery and bird watching

2. GUIDE REQUIREMENTS:
   - Professional guide REQUIRED in all Big Five reserves
   - Self-drive allowed in some parks (Kruger, Etosha) but guide strongly recommended
   - Walking safaris require armed ranger escort

3. SAFETY RULES:
   - Stay in vehicle during game drives (unless guided walking safari)
   - Maintain safe distance from wildlife
   - No flash photography — disturbs animals
   - Follow park speed limits (typically 40-50 km/h)

4. TIMING:
   - Dawn drives (05:30-09:00) and dusk drives (15:30-18:30) for best sightings
   - Midday is typically rest time — animals retreat to shade
   - Night drives available in private conservancies only

- BLOCKING: None typical (no life-threatening hard limits)
- STRONG: Guide required in Big Five reserves, seasonal migration timing, vehicle safety
- SOFT: Optimal drive timing, camera equipment, clothing color (neutral tones)
